How to play

  1. Steer. Point, or hold WASD. The bead keeps moving.
  2. Leave a line. Inside your ink you are safe. Step outside and you lay a wet line.
  3. Close the loop. Come home across your own ink to claim the ground you enclosed.
  4. Do not cross your line. Your own wet trail ends the run.
  5. Guard the trail. An enemy bead on your wet line cuts you. Their body does not. The rim does not.
  6. Take the plane. Own every cell and the plane is yours. Your best share opens new inks.
